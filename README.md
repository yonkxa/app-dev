# Alchemy of Souls Season 1
### K-Drama / Romance
Alchemy of Souls Season 1 is a fantasy historical drama set in the fictional nation of Daeho, focusing on young mages and a forbidden spell that allows souls to switch bodies.

The powerful assassin **Naksu** is fatally wounded while being pursued by the mages of Songrim. To survive, she performs the forbidden **"Alchemy of Souls"** spell, but her soul ends up trapped in the powerless, blind body of a servant girl named **Mu-deok**.

Meanwhile, **Jang Uk**, the illegitimate son of a noble family (born under the King's Star), is unable to use his powers because his father, Jang Kang, blocked his energy gates before disappearing.

**The Master-Student Bond:** Jang Uk recognizes Naksu's soul inside **Mu-deok**. He makes her his personal maid and, recognizing her true identity, convinces her to become his secret master. Mu-deok reluctantly agrees, hoping to eventually regain her power.

**Unlocking Power:** Through rigorous training and challenges, Mu-deok helps Jang Uk unlock his energy gates, enabling him to finally learn magic. Their relationship deepens as they train, taking them to new places like Danhyanggok where they meet the legendary Master Lee.

**Conflict with Jin Mu:** The main antagonist is Jin Mu, a powerful mage who is secretly practicing the forbidden soul-shifting magic and manipulating the government. Mu-deok and Jang Uk frequently cross paths with him and his dangerous soul shifters, uncovering secrets about the ancient Ice Stone, a relic that prevents soul shifters from going wild.

**Identity Revealed:** Through a series of events involving the Ice Stone, it is revealed that Mu-deok is actually Jin Bu-yeon, the long-lost daughter of Jin Ho-kyung, the head of the powerful Jin family, who was thought to have died.

**Tragic Climax:** In the season finale, Jin Mu gains control of Shaman Choi's soul-controlling bells and uses them on Mu-deok. Under this dark spell, Mu-deok is forced to go wild, killing an official and then fatally stabbing Jang Uk, her beloved student.

**Rebirth:** Jang Uk is given a criminal's funeral. However, as his body is cremated, the power of the Ice Stone inside him manifests as a blue flame, signifying his rebirth. Meanwhile, Mu-deok, regaining a moment of consciousness after attacking Jang Uk, jumps into the lake, a grave for soul shifters, only to be saved by two mysterious figures.
